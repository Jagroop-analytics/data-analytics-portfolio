# 📊 AtliQ Hardware — Sales & Finance Analytics | Excel

## 🚀 Project Overview

This project is an **end-to-end Sales & Finance Analytics solution built in Microsoft Excel**, using **Power Query, Power Pivot, Data Modeling, and DAX**.

The objective was to transform raw business data from **AtliQ Hardware** into interactive analytical reports that provide insights into **customer performance, market performance, profitability, and financial trends**.

The project demonstrates how Excel can be used beyond basic spreadsheets to perform structured data analysis and support **data-driven business decision-making**.

---

## 📈 Sales Analytics

### 1. Customer Net Sales Performance Report

Analyzed customer-level net sales performance to understand **sales contribution, growth trends, and customer performance** across different periods.

**Key analysis:**

* Customer-wise Net Sales
* Sales growth trends
* Customer contribution
* Year-over-Year performance

### 2. Market Performance vs Target Report

Compared **actual market sales against predefined targets** to evaluate performance and identify markets with significant performance gaps.

**Key analysis:**

* Actual vs Target Sales
* Performance variance
* Market-wise performance
* Identification of high- and low-performing markets

### 3. Gross Margin % by Quarter & Sub-Zone

Analyzed **Gross Margin % across quarters and sub-zones** to understand profitability patterns and variations across different business regions.

---

## 💰 Finance Analytics

### 4. P&L Statement by Fiscal Months

Created a monthly **Profit & Loss Statement** to analyze financial performance throughout the fiscal year.

**Key metrics:**

* Net Sales
* Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)
* Gross Margin
* Gross Margin %
* Operating expenses
* Net Profit

### 5. P&L Statement by Fiscal Years

Developed a fiscal-year-level P&L report to evaluate **long-term revenue and profitability trends**.

The report helps compare financial performance across fiscal years and identify changes in profitability.

### 6. P&L Statement by Market — 2021

Performed market-wise P&L analysis to understand **revenue, costs, gross margin, and profitability across different markets**.

---

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

Through these reports, the analysis focuses on:
